Privacy Policy

lucimaano uploader. Effective November 19, 2026.

Who this applies to

lucimaano uploader ("the app") is a private tool run by the owner of the lucimaano channels. The owner is its only user. The app is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What the app accesses

  • Twitch: the public live stream of the lucimaano channel, which it records to make clips.
  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the lucimaano YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope). The app does not read, change or delete other YouTube data.
  • Instagram (Meta): permission to publish Reels to the lucimaano Instagram account.
  • TikTok: basic profile information of the lucimaano TikTok account, and permission to upload and post videos to that account.
  • Telegram: a private bot that sends each clip to the owner so they can approve or skip it.

How the information is used

The app uses this access only to upload the owner's own approved gameplay clips to the owner's own accounts, and to show the owner whether each upload worked. It does not use the information for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

Where it is stored

Access tokens, recordings, clips and upload history are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server run by the app, because there is none. Information goes only to the platform it is meant for: a clip for YouTube is sent to Google, a clip for TikTok is sent to TikTok, and so on. Gameplay recordings are also sent to Google's Gemini API to find moments worth clipping.

Recordings and clips are deleted automatically, usually within hours to a few days.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share information with anyone, except for sending each clip to the platform it is being posted to.

Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies, trackers or analytics.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from each platform's own settings, such as Google Account third-party connections, TikTok's app permissions and Meta's business integrations. Stored tokens and files can be deleted from the owner's computer at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
